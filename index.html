Why Lumeli?

Work doesn’t need more noise. It needs clarity.

Every day, thousands of messages, meetings, and commitments compete for your attention. Lumeli quietly shines a light on what truly matters, so you can move through your day with confidence—and leave work behind when it’s time to live.

How it works

Meet Signals. Your commitments, in both directions.

Lumeli reads your mail and finds the promises inside it — the ones you’ve made, and the ones made to you. Each becomes a Signal: a small card with a due date, an honest time estimate, and a practical next step.

What you owe. What you’re owed.

A clay arrow pointing up means someone is waiting on you. A sea arrow pointing down means you’re waiting on someone else. Together, that’s your whole picture — so following up stops living in the back of your mind. Check a Signal off, snooze it, or dismiss it; each one carries an honest estimate, so you know what a task really costs before you begin.

Privacy

Your mail belongs on your Mac. So that’s where it stays.

Lumeli is local-first: your email is stored and indexed in a database on your own computer, not on our servers. When Lumeli looks for Signals, only the minimal excerpts needed for analysis pass through our secure relay — never stored, never logged, never used to train AI models.
